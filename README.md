⚙️ Resolution & FPS Customization

Different Android devices have different screen resolutions and display configurations.

If the boot animation does not fit your screen properly, or if the animation speed is too fast or too slow, you can adjust it through the "desc.txt" file.

The first line uses this format:

WIDTH HEIGHT FPS

For example:

1080 2400 30

- "WIDTH": Boot animation canvas width.
- "HEIGHT": Boot animation canvas height.
- "FPS": Animation playback speed in frames per second.

Important: Changing the canvas resolution does not automatically resize the animation frames. Make sure the frames are also prepared appropriately for your target resolution.

⚠️ Important: Backup Before Installation

Please back up your original stock boot animation before installing this module.

This module replaces your device's existing boot animation with a custom Aemeath boot animation.

Without a backup, your original stock boot animation may be overwritten and difficult to restore.

Where to Find the Stock Boot Animation

The location of the original "bootanimation.zip" can vary depending on your device and firmware.

Common locations include:

- "/system/media/bootanimation.zip"
- "/system/product/media/bootanimation.zip"

These are examples, not universal paths. Your device may store the file somewhere else.

Backup Instructions

1. Locate your device's original stock boot animation file.
2. Copy it to your internal storage or external storage (SD card).
3. Store the backup somewhere safe.
4. Confirm that your backup is accessible before installing this module.

«Important: Do not delete your backup after installation. Keep it in case you want to restore your original boot animation.»

The backup location and restoration method may vary depending on your device and Android firmware.
